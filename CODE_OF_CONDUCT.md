# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be respectful. Disagree about ideas, not people. Assume good faith.
Don't harass anyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Treat everyone with respect, regardless of background or experience level.
- Critique code and ideas, not the people behind them.
- Assume the person you're replying to is acting in good faith.
- If you cause harm — even unintentionally — apologise and adjust.
- Help newcomers; everyone was new once.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, insults, or sustained disruption of discussion.
- Sexualised language, imagery, or unwanted advances.
- Harassment, in public issues or in private messages.
- Publishing anyone's private information without consent.
- Sustained advocacy for any of the above.

## Scope

This applies to every space connected to the project: GitHub issues, pull
requests, code review comments, discussion threads, and any chat or event
where you're representing the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately
via the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form
(reused as a private contact channel) or directly to the maintainer.

Reports will be handled confidentially and promptly.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may, at their discretion:

- Ask the person to stop.
- Edit, hide, or delete the offending content.
- Block the person from the repository — temporarily or permanently —
  depending on severity and pattern.

Severe or repeated violations get permanent bans without warning.

## Acknowledgements

This document draws on the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) but is
deliberately shorter and direct. The substantive expectations are the same.
